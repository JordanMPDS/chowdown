ingredients:
- 1/4c Parsley
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 chicken breasts
- 2 tsp Chicken Broth Concentrate
- 2 fl. oz Marsala Wine
- 10 oz mashed potatoes
- 4 oz sliced mushrooms
- 1/2 oz flour
- 3/4 oz roasted garlic & herb butter

directions:
- Coarsely chop parsley
- Mince garlic
- Pat chicken dry.  Cover with plastic wrap and use a heavy object to pound to an even 1/8" thickness.
- Unwrap chicken and season both sides with a pinch of salt and pepper
- Place flour on a plate (reserve 1 tsp for sauce). Place chicken in flour and flip to coat both sides, pressing gently to adhere.  Repeat with second chicken breast
- Place a large non-stick pan over medium-high heat and add 1 Tbsp olive oil
- Add chicken to hot pan and cook undisturbed until browned, about 2-3 minutes on one side
- Reduce heat to medium.  Flip chicken and cook another 3-5 minutes until done
- Transfer chicken to plate and cover with foil
- Return pan to medium heat.  Add 1 tsp olive oil, garlic, and mushrooms to hot pan.  Stir occasionally until slightly softened
- Add reserved flour (1 tsp) and stir until no dry flour remains
- Add wine, chicken base, and 1 Tbsp water.  Stir to combine and bring to a simmer
- Stir occasionally until slightly thickened.  If too thick, add water to acheive desired consistency.
- Remove from burner, stir in half the butter, parsley, and pepper until combined.
- Combined mashed potatoes with remaining butter.  Stir to combine
