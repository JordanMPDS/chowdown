ingredients:
- 1 stick unsalted butter at room temperature
- 75g cane sugar
- 136g dark brown sugar
- 13g or 1Tbsp vanilla
- 1gg at room temperature
- 233g flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 215g mini chocolate chips

directions:
- Preheat oven to 350°.
- Cream butter, sugars, and vanilla.
- Add in egg, mix to combine.
- Once mixed, in same bowl add flour, baking soda, salt and mix until just incorporated.
- Mix in chocolate chips until just combined.
- Scoop into 2 Tbsp balls.
- Bake for 9-11 minutes, until just barely browning on top.
- Let rest to finish baking on sheet.
